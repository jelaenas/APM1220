**Brief description:** 
The Principal Component Analysis (PCA) was performed on seven quantitative variables from the World Happiness Report 2021 dataset: Ladder Score, Logged GDP per Capita, Social Support, Healthy Life Expectancy, Freedom to Make Life Choices, Generosity, and Perceptions of Corruption. The variables all had different measurement scales and ranges, thus they were standardized before performing PCA.

Based on the scree plot, Kaiser criterion, and cumulative proportion of variance, two principal components were retained. PC1 represents General Well-being and Quality of Life, primarily capturing Ladder Score, Logged GDP per Capita, Healthy Life Expectancy, and Social Support. Meanwhile, PC2 represents another dimension involving Freedom to Make Life Choices, Generosity and Perceptions of Corruption.

Together, the two retained components explain 74.3% of the total variance in the original seven variables. This indicates that PCA successfully reduces the dimensionality of the dataset while still managing to preserve a substantial amount of information about the underlying dimensions of cross-country well-being.
